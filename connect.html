Setting one up

You do this once.

Geedo joins your WiFi once and remembers it forever. After that every start goes straight to his face, and new animations turn up on their own. Set him up from this computer over the USB cable, or from your phone with no cable at all — either way you only do it once.

Plug him in

He turns up here when you plug him in.

Not over WiFi — a web page cannot look around your home network, and his radio is off between check-ins anyway. This is the cable, and it works on a Geedo who has never seen a network, which is exactly the one you have just taken out of a box.

Allow him once and this page knows him after that: plug him in and he appears by himself. Chrome and Edge only — Safari and Firefox cannot open serial devices at all.

Or from your phone

    


      

        

          STEP 01
          
Switch him on

          
His boot animation plays straight away, before he even looks for
             WiFi. If he's never been set up, he asks for it.

        

        
Hi! Let's set me up:
1. phone WiFi list
2. join this one:
   [ GEEDO-Setup ]
no password needed!

      


      

        

          STEP 02
          
Join GEEDO-Setup on your phone

          
Open your WiFi settings and connect to it — there's no password. A
             page opens by itself. Pick your home network, type your password,
             hit save.

          

            If the page doesn't pop up, open a browser and go to
            192.168.4.1. The setup network stays open for
            5 minutes; switch him off and on to reopen it.

          STEP 03
          
That's it

          
He connects, fetches his animations, and comes alive. From then on
             the setup screen only ever comes back if he can't reach your WiFi.

          

            Changed your WiFi password? Hold the button 5 seconds — he erases
            the old network and reopens setup. Let go early and nothing happens.

        

        

      


    

  


  

    
His screens, and what they mean

    

      

        
          
Hi! Let's set me upNo WiFi saved — join GEEDO-Setup. Also shows if his saved network stops working

          
getting my stuff…Downloading animations from the Hub

          
updating…Installing new firmware — leave him powered until he reboots himself

          
Fetch FAILEDOn your WiFi but can't reach the Hub. Usually a login-page network (school, café, hotel)

          
LOW BATTERYPlug in USB-C. He warns at most once every 90 seconds — he won't nag

          
KEEP HOLDINGYou're holding the button. Keep going to erase WiFi, or let go

        
      

      

        
The button does two things.

        

          Tap to pet him — he reacts straight away and cheers
          up a little. Keep tapping and he gets more and more wound up.
        

        

          Hold 5 seconds to erase his WiFi and start setup
          over.
        

        

          He remembers the petting. A Geedo who gets a tap every day wears a
          different set of faces than one left on a shelf — and he keeps his mood
          through a power cut and through updates. He never sulks, though, and
          never guilts you into picking him up.
        

        

          Secret: pet him ten times in a row and he sings his theme song.
        

      

    

  


  

    
Getting a pack onto him

    

      

        
Public packs need nothing from you.

        

          He checks the Hub every 60 seconds. Anything newly switched on is
          downloaded and saved to his own storage, so it keeps playing with the
          WiFi off — in the car, on a trip, or when the internet is down.
        

        

          See the packs
        

      

      

        
Got a code?

        

          Codes from a giveaway or a bought pack are redeemed on the Unlock page.
          Type it in and the pack opens in your browser — you can watch every
          animation and download each one.
        

        

          Redeem a code
        

        

          Geedo can take the code himself (firmware v27+): the setup page
          has a Pack code box, and so does the USB link above. Type it
          once and he downloads the pack, unlocks it with the same
          key-stretching the Hub uses, and keeps it in his own memory — it
          survives WiFi erases, Hub changes, and power cuts, and never needs
          the code again. He holds four packs; the page he serves on your
          network lets you remove one to make room.
        

      

    

  


  

    
If something goes wrong

    

      
        
No GEEDO-SetupSome phones hide networks with no internet. Turn WiFi off and on, or check the full list. It only runs for 5 minutes after switch-on — restart him to reopen it

        
Page won't openBrowse to 192.168.4.1 by hand

        
Fetch FAILEDNetworks with a login page block him. He needs an ordinary home network, and he'll keep retrying on his own

        
Blank screenPress RESET inside the case. If he still appears on your WiFi, the display ribbon is probably loose

        
Update failed + codeHe retries by himself, so usually just wait. The code says where it broke — the owner's guide lists them
