What do I need?

Home WiFi on 2.4 GHz, a USB-C cable to charge him, and a phone, once, to tell him your WiFi. Networks with a login page, like a school's or a hotel's, will not work.

Are the animations free?

Everything on the Hub is free and arrives by itself. Packs are extra sets that open with a code.

Does he work without WiFi?

Yes. He keeps what he has downloaded and plays it anywhere.
