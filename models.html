The lineup

One brain,
many shells.

Every Geedo — every model, every colour, every special edition — runs the same board and the same firmware. Variety lives in the layers that are cheap to change, never in the electronics.

This isn't a compromise, it's the trick. The board is the expensive, slow, risky part: it costs setup fees, takes two weeks, and has to be re-verified every time it changes. The moment a “model” needs a different board, that model costs hundreds of pounds and a month before a single one exists. Companies that forget this end up with five products they can't afford to keep making.

Anyone can make a case.

The case spec is published on purpose. You can design a Geedo shell without owning a board — and every case someone designs makes Geedo better without costing anything to make.

That's the honest answer to “I don't want to make five products”: the best variety is variety you didn't have to build. The Hub animations already work this way, and cases can too.
