Got a code?

From a giveaway, a pack you bought, or a friend. Type it in and the pack opens right here.

Codes look like GD-X7K9-PQ4M. Dashes optional; older 6-letter codes still work.

Download for Geedo
To put it on your Geedo: hold his button for 5 seconds, join GEEDO-Setup on your phone like the first time, and type this same code into the Pack code box. He downloads and unlocks it himself, keeps it in his own memory forever, and plays his gift ceremony when it lands. Needs firmware v27.

Your code is never stored on the Hub.

Not the code, not a hash of it. Every pack sits there as a locked box and the code you type is the key, which is why a wrong one simply doesn't open anything, and why the check takes a moment.
