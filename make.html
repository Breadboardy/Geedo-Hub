How it works

Four steps. Only the first one is hard.

Draw it in the Studio

A pixel editor in your browser: pencil, shapes, bucket, text, frames with onion skin, and you can drop a photo in and turn it into pixels. Press Play to see it move. Save keeps a .geedo.json file on your computer so you can come back to it.

Press Publish

Studio saves your file and opens the submission form on GitHub. Give it a name, drop the file in, tick the three rules, send. No GitHub account? See the bottom of this page.

A person looks at it

The bot posts a preview and the numbers on your submission within a minute. Then we look at it, always, usually within a day. Kids watch these screens, so nothing goes out that nobody has seen. If it is a no, you are told why, and you can fix it and send it again.

Watch him do it

Approved means it is on the Hub with your name on it and its own page. Every Geedo checks the Hub once a minute and downloads anything new, so yours is doing it within the minute.

The rules

Three you tick, one the bot measures.

✏️

Your own drawing

Not a character from a game, a film or a show, and not somebody else's pixel art. Geedo's own face is fair game: remix anything on the Hub.

💛

Kind

Nothing rude, mean or scary. A Geedo lives on somebody's desk, and a lot of those somebodies are kids.

🕶️

Not flashy

No more than three big flashes in any second. Fast flashing can hurt people. The bot measures it, and Geedo himself refuses to play anything over the line.

📦

Small

Up to 96 frames and under 32 KB once packed. His memory is tiny and every Geedo keeps every animation, so short and bold beats long and busy.

Good to know

Screen
128 × 64 pixels, on or off. No grey, no colour.
Speed
1 to 30 frames a second, and any frame can hold for longer.
Plays
Looping, ping-pong, or once through.
Files
.geedo.json is your Studio project. .bin is what the robot plays; the Studio can save that too.

Remix anything

Every animation page has Open in Studio. It loads those frames into the editor, and when you publish your version it goes up with a line saying what it started from, and a link back.
