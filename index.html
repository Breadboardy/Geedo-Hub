how it works

He joins your WiFi once

Switch him on, connect your phone to GEEDO-Setup and pick your network. He remembers it, and every start after that goes straight to his face.

New animations arrive on their own

He checks the Hub every few minutes and keeps whatever is new in his own memory, so it is still there after he has been switched off.

You can draw your own

Geedo Studio is a pixel editor in your browser. Draw something, publish it, and watch your robot start doing it.
