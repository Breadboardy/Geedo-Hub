How it works

He joins your WiFi once

Switch him on, connect your phone to GEEDO-Setup, and pick your network. He remembers it forever. Every start after that goes straight to his face.

New animations arrive on their own

He checks the Hub every minute. Anything new reaches every Geedo in the world within 60 seconds, and he saves it to his own memory so it survives power cuts.

You can draw your own

Geedo Studio is a pixel editor in your browser. Draw something, publish it, and watch your robot start doing it.

Animation packs Browse packs →

A box of new tricks.

Packs are themed sets that arrive on your Geedo together: ocean, stickman, space. Some are public and land on every robot. Others come with a code, and only your robot gets them.

Codes are never stored on our servers, not even scrambled. Each pack is sealed with real encryption, and the code you type is the only key.
