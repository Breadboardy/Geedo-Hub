How a pack gets to your robot

Public packs

Switched on for everyone. Geedo notices at his next check and downloads them — you do nothing at all, and the new-pack celebration plays when they land.

Code packs

Bought one, or won one in a giveaway? You get a code like GD-K7X9-PQ4M. Enter it and the pack is yours.

A code is a key, not a password.

The Hub never stores your code — not the code, not even a hash of it. Every private pack sits on the Hub as a locked box, and the code you type is what opens it. Get it wrong and the box simply doesn't open; there's nothing to look up and nothing to read off.

The key is stretched through 600,000 rounds of PBKDF2 before it's tried, which is why the Unlock page pauses for a moment. That pause is the point: it makes guessing codes hundreds of thousands of times more expensive, and costs you about half a second.
