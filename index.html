02 / How it works

He joins your WiFi once

Switch him on, connect your phone to GEEDO-Setup, pick your network. He remembers it forever — every start after that goes straight to his face.

He checks the Hub every minute

New animation published? It's on every Geedo on Earth within 60 seconds, and saved to his own storage so it survives a power cut.

You draw your own

Geedo Studio is a 128×64 pixel editor in the browser. Draw, publish, and watch it turn up on the robot.

03 / One brain, many shells All models →

Every Geedo — every model, every colour, every special edition — runs the same board and the same firmware. Variety lives in the layers that are cheap to change, never in the electronics.

It's how phone cases work, and Lego minifigures. The board is the slow, expensive, risky part; the moment a “model” needs a different one, that model costs hundreds of pounds and a month before a single one exists.
